The Brazil-Nut Box

Bury the heavy bead at the bottom, then SHAKE the box — and it climbs to the top, against gravity, against its own weight. That's the Brazil-nut effect. The climb is no trick of animation: the bead rests on the tallest of its support columns, and a column can only drop the bead when every one of them opens a void at once — rare. So the bead ratchets up and resists sinking. Turn off the shake and it freezes flat; shrink the bead to grain-size and the climb dies. Grab the box and shake it.
