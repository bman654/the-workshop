Drag a grommet across the pegboard — a wheel rolls the edge, the dots count inside.

One rubber polygon, two roads to its area. The brass wheel rolls the boundary — Green's theorem, calculus: A = L·ΔW. The lit dots count themselves — Pick's theorem, pure counting: A = I + B/2 − 1. Both needles settle on the same number.

Drag any grommet to reshape (mouse or touch). A move only commits if the polygon stays simple — Pick needs that. Off-lattice (break the lattice, or free-drag with Pin off) the count road greys out — Pick is UNDEFINED — while the wheel stays true (Green works on any curve). The Hole preset is a static two-ring demo: A = I + B/2 − 1 + h.
