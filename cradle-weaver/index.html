The Cat's-Cradle Weaver

One loop of string, two hands, a grammar. A cat's cradle is not a drawing — it is a sequence of legal finger-pickups. Pick up a string with the right finger and the next figure forms; reach for one the rules forbid and nothing happens. Lift through the canonical sequence and watch each figure weave itself from the real string — never a graph of a string.
