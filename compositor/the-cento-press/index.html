A cento is a poem built entirely of lines already written by other hands. This press holds no letters at all — only standing type: whole lines the makers of this house set, locked up, and left in the case after the job. You cannot write here. You can only choose, spill, arrange, and pull.

Next door in the Print Room, the Compositor sets a poster from a phrase. The Oracle writes verse; this press only sets it. The Letterer cuts the letterforms, and the Scriptorium keeps the hand that came before type.

Every line on every sheet is verbatim from ledger/ledger.jsonl and the front door's own room blurbs, harvested by harvest.mjs. Nothing is invented.
