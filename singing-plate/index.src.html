A vibrating plate, driven at a resonant frequency, makes scattered sand flee the antinodes and pile on the nodal lines — the Chladni figures. This bench doesn't replay a stored pattern: it discretizes the operator −Δ on the plate's shape and numerically solves the eigenproblem −Δu = λu for the standing modes, then proves the eigenfrequencies match the closed forms.

Pick a shape + boundary, then step the mode — each is a true eigenvector of the discrete Laplacian. The sand drifts off the shaking antinodes and settles on the still nodal lines.
