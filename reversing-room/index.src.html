What you crank vs. what is proven. The crank scrubs a recorded ribbon of states for a smooth picture — it does not recompute the inverse on every frame. The proof that reverse is a law lives in the pill above: the same core.mjs inlined here runs its analytic unstep — genuine inverse physics, storing zero positions — and re-derives states it never saw. The two share one law, so the picture cannot drift from the proof.

Honest scope. The table is axis-aligned lanes: equal-mass discs on shared rows/columns, every collision a 1-D elastic exchange — so every contact time stays a ratio of integers and the arithmetic is exact forever. A free-angle disc collision would need a √ and would break bit-exactness; we never do it. Energy is the clean invariant — exactly conserved at every event, both directions. Momentum is conserved across every pair collision; a wall is external (it reverses one component), so the |p| gauge tracks pair-momentum, not a wall hit.
