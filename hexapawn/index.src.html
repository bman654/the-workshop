Hexapawn: three pawns a side on a 3×3 board. You're gold and move first; the machine is teal. Push straight into an empty square or capture one step diagonally. Reach the far rank, take every enemy, or leave them stuck — and you win.

The machine has no strategy. Each position it meets gets a little matchbox of coloured beads — one bead per legal reply. It draws a bead at random to move. When it loses, it throws that game's beads away. Beat it a few times, then try to win again — watch it run out of ways to lose.
