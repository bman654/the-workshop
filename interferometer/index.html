The claim

Light down the moving arm travels it twice, so each ring swallowed at the center is half a wavelength of travel: Δd = N·λ/2, hence λ = 2·Δd/N. A self-test proves the round-trip factor of 2 exact and rejects the naïve law (which forgets it) by ×2.
