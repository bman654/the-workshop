colophon — the one bench that builds the rest

The Drawing Room's other engines are each ONE fixed machine. This is the bench they were assembled on: drop pin-joints and rigid bars, mark a driver and a pen, and a general planar-mechanism solver articulates whatever you built. There is no formula plotted point-by-point — the curve is a theorem of the mechanism, forced by the geometry, and every bar holds its length to machine precision while it turns.

The sole authority is mechanism-bench/core.mjs (DOM-free, pure): the Newton solver (Gauss–Newton on bar-length residuals, adaptive Levenberg, a backtracking line-search, and branch tracking — a per-joint orientation sign the solver refuses to flip, so the locus can't flip mid-crank), the assembly model, and a self-contained copy of the Roberts–Chebyshev cognate closed-form (the reusable kernel lives in tools/linkage/linkage.js). It is forge-inlined byte-for-byte between the sentinels on this page and proved by a headless Node twin. The green pill reruns the SAME self-test the twin proves: ① the cognate theorem — three four-bars draw the byte-identical curve (three pens coincide to ~1e-15, every cognate bar rigid); ② Peaucellier — the pen's locus fits a line to machine-ε and the inversion product |OP|·|OQ| = L²−ℓ² holds constant; ③ the BRIDGE — a sandbox-assembled four-bar's Newton-solved locus matches the trusted closed-form to ~1e-15 while every bar stays rigid across the full sweep; ④ branch tracking — across a full rotation of both presets every fold holds, and the guard provably rejects a flip.
