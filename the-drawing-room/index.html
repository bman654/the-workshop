The Mechanism Bench

The bench the other engines were assembled on. Drop pin-joints, lay rigid bars, anoint a driver and a pen — or load a preset — then RELEASE the crank and a general planar-mechanism solver folds the whole truss live while the pen inks a locus forced by the geometry. The mobility meter falls 4→3→2→1 as bars snap on; the crank gate stays locked until it is genuinely 1-DOF. Branch-tracking keeps the locus from flipping mid-crank, and the green pill proves the cognate theorem, Peaucellier's line, and the bridge to the trusted closed-form — exact to machine precision.

The Gathering Rail

The Drawing Room gathers the estate's drawing engines — each a linkage whose figure is forced by its geometry. The Mechanism Bench above is the one you assemble freely; these are the finished machines it can build — and at the far end, the room's own drafting passport, drawn not with bars but with a route. One dark sibling is framed for the wing to grow into.
