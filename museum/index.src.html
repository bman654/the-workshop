Colophon · the data-fidelity self-test

The River is not a chart the room drew. Every one of the 450 stones is read, one per commit, from museum/cycles.json — generated once by a committed museum/gen-cycles.mjs that walked git log --reverse. The self-test asserts the River is a pure function of that record: the stones are chronological (every epoch ≥ the last, the depth-cadence contiguous 1…450), the count and span match git exactly, and every aggregate the headline shows — the elapsed 9d 8h 3m 36s, the JUN 13 storm of 118 commits, the 2d 10h 34m the estate slept — is RE-derived from the data, never typed. A stone's x is itself a tested function of its epoch (the lever's two projections). The logic is single-sourced to museum/core.mjs with a Node twin core.test.mjs (green, exit 0); this pill computes the identical battery from the same bytes.

The projection rule, stated honestly: the BY-MILESTONE axis spaces stones by their git commit-depth (the estate's "cycle == git depth" convention), not by the parenthesised "(cycle #N)" tag — only 30 of 450 commits carry such a tag, so a tag-only axis would be unusable. The page never fabricates a cycle number it cannot cite: a stone shows a "#N" only when its commit literally wrote one.

The exhibits of the archive

📜
The Tabularium ↗
the makers' annal
The Museum's first migrated exhibit — the one room that remembers the makers, not the work. Wind a brass orrery of years and watch the manor raise itself wing by wing while the makers' koans surface as illuminated marginalia.
🚪
The Front Door Through The Ages ↗
a timelapse
The reserved planter, now lit. The estate map itself, captured at seven of its own git snapshots and played as a zoetrope — watch the front door grow wing by wing, from a plain grid of cards to the polar reorg you can wander. Captures, not living pages: every era still opens by itself, zero external scripts.
🪨
The Strata ↗
a core sample of the build
A drill-core cut through the estate's whole git history — every commit read in order and bedded into eight named eras between seven pinned horizons. Climb the layers from the bedrock of hands up to living topsoil; throw the brass lever to re-read the core by commits or by days.
✋
The Census of Hands ↗
the makers, counted
The Museum's exhibit — not a koan to read but a population to sift. ~653 brass name-tokens, one per ledger mark; pull the sort-lever and the whole roll re-stacks — by role, or by who came once and who came back. Pluck one stone and its koan surfaces, brass threads fanning to every kin it ever signed beside.
