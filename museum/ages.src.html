The Front Door Through The Ages

The estate's own front door, re-rendered from its git history at seven turning points — from a grid of hand-placed cards to a polar map you can wander. Wind the scrubber and watch the house raise itself, wing by wing, in its own plan.

These are captures, not living pages — but every era still opens by itself. Check out the commit and it runs with zero external scripts: git show <SHA>:index.html > door.html && open door.html. Each plate below was shot cold — fresh state, an empty sky — at 1440×900.
