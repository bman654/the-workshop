A population of competing strategies evolves by the replicator equation: a strategy that beats the population average grows, one below it shrinks, and the mix x always sums to 1. In Hawk–Dove the Hawk fraction settles exactly at x*=V/C; in Rock–Paper–Scissors the mix circles the centre forever — the node and the ring, on one triangle.

fᵢ=(A·x)ᵢ   φ=xᵀAx   ẋᵢ=xᵢ(fᵢ−φ)  ·  Σẋᵢ=φ−φ=0 ⇒ Σxᵢ≡1

Trace it with the truthful integrator (RK4) and Σxᵢ holds at 1 to machine precision while the relative entropy D(x*‖x) falls monotonically to 0; flip to a careless coarse step and the sum still holds — but a frequency is driven provably below zero, an impossible population. The break is positivity, not the sum.
