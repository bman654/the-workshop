The Loop & the Field

Push a current through the wire loop hanging between the horseshoe poles. The field shoves the two long sides the opposite way — a couple — and the loop turns. Crank the current up to shove harder. Drag the loop to feel the restoring torque: zero at the dead spots, hardest at the flat.

The Switches

Commutator flips the current sense every half-turn so the couple always pushes the same way — OFF it only rocks and stalls, ON it spins forever. Reverse current and reverse field each flip the spin — same flip, different cause. No-commutator claim asserts the verdict: without the commutator, net work over a turn is zero.
