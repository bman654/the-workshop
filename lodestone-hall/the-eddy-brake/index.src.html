The Two Tubes

Two clear tubes, one slug each. The left tube is plastic — a plain iron slug falls through it free, gravity-time. The right tube is copper — drop the SAME slug, but magnetised, and it drifts down in slow motion though nothing touches it. The falling magnet's flux through each ring of wall is CHANGING — so an eddy current swirls, and (Lenz) it always opposes the change: the ring ahead pushes up, the ring behind pulls back. A brake made of pure induction.
