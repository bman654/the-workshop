A Pendulum Made of Electricity

A charged capacitor (left) is a coiled spring; a coil carrying current (right) is a flywheel. Wire them in a loop and the charge q plays position, the current i = dq/dt plays velocity. Charge the plates, let go — the charge pours into the coil as current, the current pours back onto the OTHER plate, forever.
