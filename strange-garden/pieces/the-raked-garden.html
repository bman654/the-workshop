The Raked Garden

A dry garden of combed sand. Drag the rake and parallel grooves follow your stroke; set a stone and the grooves part around it in quiet rings. Sweep flat to begin again — or walk away and return to the garden you left. Kin to the Marbling Bath: there you keep the ink, here you keep the sand.
