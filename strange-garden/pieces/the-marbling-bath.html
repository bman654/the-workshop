Marbling Bath

A still tank of size-thickened water. Drop concentric ink rings, then rake a comb through them — the ink rides the stroke into the feathered veins of marbled endpaper. Lift the paper to keep your print. Its reversible cousin, The Unstirring, smears a dye that comes home.
