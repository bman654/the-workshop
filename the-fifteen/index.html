The Fifteen

The unsolvable twin

Trade two tiles by hand — the one thing a slide can never do — and the seal flips. From there no sequence of slides can ever sort the board.
The board is sealed (P = 0) — solvable.
A board's area of arrangements splits cleanly in two by one bit. Every legal slide stays on the P = 0 side — so the orbit you can reach by sliding is exactly the solvable half, and GOAL lives there. A single hand-swap crosses to the P = 1 side, and no slide can ever cross back. The badge proves the bit holds across thousands of legal slides and flips on every swap.
