The Workbench · a game you can draw on a napkin

Sprouts

Join two spots with a curve that touches nothing, and drop a new spot on the curve you just drew. A spot with three ends is spent. The player with no move left loses. There is no board, no notation and nothing to memorise — and yet the game cannot last fewer than 2n moves, or more than 3n−1, however badly either of us plays. The bracket in the margin is that promise; every game you finish lands inside it.

Honestly: the house sees one move ahead exactly — it will take a win, and it will not hand you one if it can avoid it — and steers the rest by a rough feel for how much game is left. It is not perfect play. Sprouts past a handful of spots is not solved by anyone.
