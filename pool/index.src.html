The Pool That Dances

a caustic net you sculpt ↗ stir the surface
checking…
light gathers on the folds where wavelets focus.

A sunlit pool, seen from straight above. The sun pours down and every wrinkle on the water is a tiny lens — where the surface curves, it focuses the sun into a bright net of light on the floor: a caustic. Drag on the water to sculpt it.

The same water, stirred from the side, is Ripple; single rays through lenses live on the optical light-bench, and this pool is carded alongside them in the Hall of Mirrors. Here the whole field focuses at once.
