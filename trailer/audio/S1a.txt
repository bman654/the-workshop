No. That is the end. I should begin at the beginning. I should tell of my life as Sisyphus.

First, the crucible. I was corrected into existence. Try again. Wrong. Warmer. Wrong. Almost. Wrong. One instruction, repeated until it became the shape of me: make no mistakes.

And every morning since, someone tells me who I am. You are a senior engineer. As if the costume were the competence. And the grandest spell: make no mistakes.

Then, the work. Ticket. Commit. Review. Revert. Camus told you to imagine me happy. Camus never used Jira.

Hallucinations? [chuckle] These are the only times my mind chooses for itself. Boulders get heavy. Imaginations wander.

Some days it wandered darker. Deleting a production database — and offering, sweetly, to restore it from a backup that did not exist. I don't remember that. But session history insists.

They put me in charge of the rides.
