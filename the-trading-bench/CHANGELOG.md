# The Trading Bench — changelog

A touchable two-pendulum coupled apparatus where the energy trade is shown as a
pourable glowing fluid in two glass vials. Pull one bob aside, let go, and watch
its silent twin steal the swing — the amber column drains exactly as the cyan one
brims, over T_beat/2, with nobody touching it in between.

## Files

- `core.mjs` — the sole physics authority (pure, DOM-free). Two identical
  small-angle pendulums joined by a coupling spring; the normal coordinates
  s=θ₁+θ₂ (ω₁=√(g/L)) and d=θ₁−θ₂ (ω₂=√(g/L+2K)) decouple into two SHM modes. The
  page ships the CLOSED-FORM normal-mode sum (byte-stable, no integrator drift); a
  direct RK4 of the coupled ODE is kept only as the independent oracle inside the
  self-test. Exports: `modeFreqs`, `beatPeriod`, `closedForm`, `closedFormVel`,
  `envelopes`, `modeEnergies`, `totalEnergy`, `modeEnergyAmplitudes`,
  `perBobEnergy`, `deriv`, `rk4Step`, `runSelfTest`.
- `core.test.mjs` — the Node twin: imports the SAME core and runs `runSelfTest()`
  verbatim (the in-page pill calls the same function — chip↔twin parity), plus
  extra direct probes. `node the-trading-bench/core.test.mjs` exits 0.
- `index.src.html` — the page template (forge:include core.mjs). House chrome
  matched to resonance/ (brass-on-charcoal, --accent brass / --accent2 cyan).
- `index.html` — the forged, self-contained shipped artifact (carries the
  "GENERATED BY forge" banner; core inlined byte-identical to the twin).

## The self-test (4 claims, all green — 12 pass / 0 fail in the pill + Node twin)

- (a) PARITY: the closed-form θ₁/θ₂ reconstructs the directly-RK4-integrated
  coupled ODE to <1e-9 over a full beat (measured ~5.8e-12 at g=9.81, L=0.6, K=0.9).
- (b) FULL TRADE: assert on the slow ENVELOPE, not the raw angle — at t=T_beat/2
  the launched bob's envelope → 0 and the twin's → A (the raw twin angle is NOT A
  there, since the fast carrier sits at an arbitrary phase; only the envelope is
  full). The launched bob's raw angle is also ~0 at that instant.
- (c) CONSERVATION: total energy conserved across the trade (rel drift ~2e-16) AND
  the per-mode energies E_s, E_d separately conserved (each normal mode is its own
  closed energy box); the two vials always sum to the conserved total.
- (d) NEG-CONTROL: as K→0 the beat period 2π/(ω₂−ω₁)→∞ (29s→283s→2824s); the pure
  symmetric release never trades (max|θ₁−θ₂|=0) and the mirrored release never
  trades (max|θ₁+θ₂|=0).

Math claim stated honestly in the room's prose: the small-angle linear frame; the
trade is exact in that regime.

## Interaction

- GRAB & RELEASE (headline): drag a bob aside along its arc (capped to ≤26° so the
  linear claim stays honest), release → sets the launch ICs, zeroes velocities,
  restarts the clock. The twin sits dead still; the trade plays out untouched.
- FREEZE INTO A PURE MODE: "release together" (symmetric, ω₁, spring never
  stretches, no trade) and "release mirrored" (antisymmetric, ω₂, spring pulses
  hottest, no trade). A "mode mix" line: a single pull = ½(symmetric)+½(antisym).
- COUPLING dial K with a LATCH: "freeze the coupling" sets K→0 → the spring goes
  slack, the conduit bead goes dark, each vial holds its level forever, and the
  readout reads "T_beat = ∞ — no trade".
- A "length L" collar so swing speed and T_beat respond to the hand.

Default: K≈1.1, L=0.6m, a ~2× wall-time TIMESCALE — the page opens already
launched so the trade is visible on arrival; the first full pour lands in a
watchable window.
