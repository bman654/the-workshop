▸ why it can't be a draw

R(3,3) = 6 — the no-draw theorem

Colour every one of the 32768 ways to 2-colour K6's 15 chords, and check each for a same-colour triangle. Exactly 0 escape — every complete colouring hides a monochromatic triangle. So the board can never fill without a loser. There is no draw to be had.

Five studs can escape: of the 1024 ways to 2-colour K5, 12 are triangle-free. The cleanest is the pentagon-and-pentagram split — one 5-cycle in each colour, neither holding a triangle. Six is the exact threshold where escape becomes impossible.

K5 escapes. K6 cannot.
