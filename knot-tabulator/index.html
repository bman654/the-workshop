What this bench proves — & doesn't

PROVEN here: stability (the determinant is byte-identical on a random Reidemeister walk over THESE diagrams — not a proof for all knots), discrimination (det 3≠1; trefoil 3-colourable, the unknot not), and the teeth (crossing count changes under R-I).

Stated, not re-derived: invariance under ALL Reidemeister moves for ALL diagrams is a theorem (Alexander/Goeritz); the bench CHECKS it on a random walk over 4 diagrams.

The one-way caveat: det ≠ 1 ⟹ knotted (sound — the unknot has det 1). The converse is FALSE — some knots also read 1 — so this proves knottedness, never unknottedness. The determinant is also blind to mirror image (left & right trefoil both read 3): it proves a loop knotted, it does not classify it.

Scope: one bench, one invariant, four hard-coded diagrams + a verified move-applier — deliberately not a freehand editor or a topology wing. Jones polynomials, the knot group, draw-your-own: each a fresh seed, not an in-place expansion.
