the pressure wave p′ and the heat pulse q′ set the sign of γ.
Rayleigh’s criterion. A flame feeds the standing wave only when it gives its heat where the air is being compressed. For a gauze at fractional height u = xh/L in an open–open tube the growth rate reduces to γ ∝ Q·sin(2π·u): it sings in the lower half (0<u<½), falls silent in the upper half (½<u<1), and is dead-zero exactly at u=½. Heat→0 silences it everywhere.
