Four Beats to a Turn

Two cylinders, each open at both ends, quartered a quarter-turn apart. That is four exhausts every revolution — so the beat you hear is the wheel going round, and the sound of a locomotive is a speedometer with no dial in it: beats/s = 4v / πD. Open the regulator. Then hang it in full gear on greasy rail and listen to the one moment the arithmetic breaks.

three clocks, one crank

Every tick is one exhaust valve opening, at the crank angle loco.mjs put it at — not on a grid. The tall tick is one turn of the wheel: watch the chalk mark on the near driver come round with it. Even spacing is the quartering — knock it out and the engine limps.
