Three felt rows of brass stones. On your turn, sweep away any number of stones from one row — press the stone where your take begins and drag, or just click a stone to take it and everything past it. Take the last stone to win. The flint opponent answers with provably perfect play, read straight from a fully-solved table. It will feel uncanny. Lose twice and it will show you exactly how it reads the board.

Every verdict is read from the same proven table the green chip verifies — the page and the headless proof share one solver. With the balance shown, each row splits into binary place-columns (4s · 2s · 1s); a column-lamp lights when an odd number of rows have a stone there. All lamps dark = a balanced board, and whoever faces it loses.
