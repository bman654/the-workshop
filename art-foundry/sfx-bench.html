Art Foundry — SFX Render Bench — dev harness, not forged, not shipped

A GENERALIZED lift of the-gate/audio-bench.html: instead of loading audio-<key>.js by key, it loads ONE candidate.js (the WebAudio module under test, copied in beside this page by render-wav.sh) which registers a builder onto window.Gate.sfx. Call renderCandidate(durSec) from the console or via agent-browser eval: it renders the candidate through an OfflineAudioContext at 22050 Hz, encodes a 16-bit mono PCM WAV, drops the base64 into #wavout, sets window.__wavB64, and RESOLVES the base64 string (agent-browser eval awaits the promise).
