A star is a standoff: gravity pulls every gram inward forever, and only pressure pushes back. Where they cancel is the star's rest radius. Grab the limb and squeeze — you over-stuff the pressure, it shoves back, overshoots, and the whole body rings until it settles right back where it started. Turn the brass fusion knob to change how stiff the gas is and the rest radius moves. Or cut the pressure entirely: nothing is left to balance gravity, and the star just falls.
