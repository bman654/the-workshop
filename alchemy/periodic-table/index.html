The Aufbau Staircase

The periodic table isn't drawn here — it is born, one electron at a time, from a single rule: fill subshells by increasing (n + ℓ), ties broken by n. Press STEP to drop the next electron; watch it climb the brass diagonals, the matching cell ignite, and the noble gases flare as the periods seal at {2, 10, 18, 36}. Then flip to the naïve "n only" order and watch the same atom build a broken table — proof that the table's whole silhouette is caused by n + ℓ, and nothing else.
