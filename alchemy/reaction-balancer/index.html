The Reaction Balancer

Atoms are never made or destroyed across the arrow. The whole-number coefficients that make a reaction true are not guessed — they are read off the nullspace of the element-count matrix. Pick a reaction; watch the molecules build on the two pans of the balance, and the beam settle level only when every element's atom-tally is equal, left and right.

per-element gauges show the matrix

The two pans never weigh grams — they weigh atoms, element by element. A reaction balances exactly when, for every element, the count on the left equals the count on the right. That is one linear equation per element, and the coefficients are the kernel they all share. Some species genuinely cannot be reconciled — the negative control tips the beam and the solver returns nothing rather than fake a level. The atoms you reassemble here are the same ones the Cavern takes apart.

The math lives in core.mjs, inlined byte-identical between sentinels & self-tested in Node.  ·  Back to the Alchemy Lab
