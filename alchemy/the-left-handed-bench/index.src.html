The Left-Handed Bench

Turn the molecule in your hands. Press SEAT and it rotates to its best fit in the mirror socket — and stays splayed. A reflection is the move your hand cannot make.

Your two hands, and the mirror you can never cross. Its kin, the Crystal Garden, grows; this one refuses.
