Le Chatelier's Vise

Seal N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ in a glass cylinder. It runs both ways at once and settles where forward and back exactly cancel — that's equilibrium, where the quotient Q equals the constant K. Now shove the piston down: you don't restart the reaction, you move the place it settles TO. The bands re-settle toward the side with fewer gas molecules and the system pushes back. Turn up the flame on this exothermic reaction and the ammonia backs off. Switch to the equal-moles reaction and the squeeze does nothing — proof the shift is real, not cosmetic. The curve at the bottom is only the shadow; the cylinder is the thing.
