The Bomb That Tells On Itself

A single photon can tell you a bomb is live without ever touching it. With the arms empty, this interferometer is tuned so the photon always exits the bright port — the dark port is forbidden. Drop a bomb (a perfect detector: any photon that reaches it goes BOOM) into one arm and fire photons one at a time. Most either blow it up or land bright — but ¼ of the time the forbidden dark port clicks, and a dark click is impossible without a bomb. That click proves the bomb is live, and the photon that proved it took the empty arm and never went near it.
