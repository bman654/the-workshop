Pull back the end ball and let it fall. The impact runs invisibly down the line and the ball on the far end leaps out alone — never two, never half-speed. That is the only outcome that conserves momentum and kinetic energy at once. Lift two and two swing out; lift three, three. The line keeps both books balanced.
