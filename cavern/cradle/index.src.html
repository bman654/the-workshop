Try it: drag any ball aside and let go, or click a ball to lift that many from its end. At e = 1 momentum balances exactly at every click and the energy line holds nearly level — a real desk toy's slow, honest wind-down, not perpetual motion. Pull e down and the energy line stair-steps down faster at each click, momentum still exact. Make one end ball heavy and the tidy "k out" rule breaks, yet both ledgers stay closed.
