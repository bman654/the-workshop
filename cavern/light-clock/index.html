A photon bounces straight up and down between two mirrors — one round trip is one tick. Set the clock moving and the photon must travel a longer, slanted path to keep up, so each tick takes longer. The same clock, ticking slow, just because it moves. That stretch is the Lorentz factor γ, and it falls straight out of Pythagoras.
