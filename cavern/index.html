The Cavern

The estate's physics laboratory, quarried underground and kept apart from the house — the dangerous experiments quarantined below for safety. The shaft forks into two drifts: a warm-lamplit Newtonian one and a cold, star-lit Einsteinian one. Every bench is a single self-contained instrument that proves its own physics exact.

Dangerous physics · handle with care

The Newtonian Drift

force · momentum · the old certainties

The lamplit gallery, where the world runs on Newton's clockwork: balls collide and nothing is lost, and the curve of fastest descent turns out to be a wheel's own track.

⚙️
Newton's Cradle
Lift a ball, let it fall; the click-clacks carry momentum and energy across the line untouched. Vary the count, the masses, the bounce.
conserves p & KE to 1e-12 🛝
The Brachistochrone on the workbench
Race four beads from A to B — and the cycloid wins. Its twin the tautochrone drops beads from any height and they all arrive together. (Built earlier; it lives on the Workbench, but it belongs here too.)
the cycloid is provably fastest

The Einsteinian Drift

light · spacetime · the deep dark

The cold gallery, deeper in, where the certainties bend: a clock made of light runs slow as it moves, and gravity nudges a planet's orbit forever onward.

⏱️
The Light Clock
Push a bouncing-photon clock toward the speed of light and watch it slow. Time dilation, length contraction, and the invariant interval — drawn from one slanted ray.
γ from Pythagoras, exact ☿
Mercury's Precession
Newton's orbit closes; Einstein's does not. Dial in the relativistic correction and watch the perihelion creep — the famous 43″ a century that first proved General Relativity.
lands on 43″/century

The Quantum Drift

the very small · superposition · the watched answer

Past where the certainties bent, the cave goes stranger still: here a single thing travels every path at once, and the simple act of looking changes what happens. The deepest, most dangerous physics in the estate — and, as ever, each bench proves it exact.

🎯
The Double Slit
Fire particles one at a time at a pair of slits; each lands as a single dot, yet the dots pile into bright and dark bands. Close a slit — or merely watch which one it takes — and the bands vanish. The experiment Feynman called the whole mystery of quantum mechanics.
fringe spacing λL/d, exact · Born-rule |ψ|² ⛰️
Quantum Tunnelling
Roll a particle at a wall it hasn't the energy to climb. Classically it always bounces back — but the wavefunction leaks through, and a sliver comes out the far side. Widen the wall and the leak dies exponentially; that fragility is why α-decay half-lives span 24 orders of magnitude.
transmission T closed-form == transfer-matrix solve · R + T = 1 exact 📦
Particle in a Box
Trap a particle between two walls it cannot pass and its energy turns quantized — a ladder of rungs spaced by n², with no rung at zero, so even at rest it must hum. Climb the rungs, count the nodes, then mix two and watch the bound packet slosh. The root of why atoms have lines, not a smear.
ladder E_n ∝ n² == a from-scratch eigensolve · orthonormal to 1e−15 🌀
The Harmonic Oscillator
Soften the box into a parabolic bowl — a quantum spring. The energy is still a ladder, but now the rungs are perfectly even (spaced by one quantum ℏω, the lowest still ½ above zero), and the wave leaks past the walls into ground a classical ball could never reach. Launch a coherent state and a quantum packet swings like a pendulum, never spreading.
even ladder E_n = ℏω(n+½) == a from-scratch eigensolve · coherent state swings classically 🕳️
The Finite Well
Give the box a real, finite depth — walls a particle can climb. Now the ladder is finite (a well holds only so many rungs; deepen it and more snap in, shallow it and the top ones evaporate — but it always binds at least one), and the wave leaks out through the walls and decays into the forbidden ground beyond, the shallowest rung reaching farthest. The realistic well, between the box and the bowl.
finite ladder from a transcendental match == a from-scratch eigensolve · exponential leak ⛓️
The Lattice
Now line the wells up in a row, repeated forever — a crystal. The atom's sharp levels can no longer stay sharp: each one smears into a band of allowed energies, and between the bands open forbidden gaps no electron may have. Where the bands and gaps fall is the whole reason a solid is a metal, a semiconductor, or an insulator.
band edges where |f(E)|≤1 == ½·tr M(E) transfer-matrix · N states per band from a ring eigensolve 🔔
Hear the Ladder
You've climbed two ladders — the box's rungs spreading as n², the oscillator's perfectly even. Now listen to them. Voiced as sound, the oscillator's even rungs are the overtone series of a single note, so they fuse into one pure pitch — that is why it is called harmonic. The box's stretching rungs never line up, so it rings like a struck bell. The same physics you saw, finally heard.
oscillator inharmonicity == 0 (one tone) · box stack provably stretches (a bell) · the Sound Garden meets the Cavern
