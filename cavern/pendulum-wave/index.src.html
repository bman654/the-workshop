The Pendulum Wave

fifteen threads slide out of phase, and back
checking…
a periodic pendulum wave — no theorem, only craft

Fifteen pendulums, each thread a hair shorter than the last, are let go from the same angle at the same instant. Their periods stand in whole-number ratio, so they drift apart into a travelling wave, scatter into seeming chaos, briefly braid into a two-strand snake — and then, all together, fall back into one crest. Then they do it again, forever.

Tempo
Play

Touch it: drag any bob aside and release to perturb the wave — it will tangle, then heal itself back into phase at the next recurrence. Each bob carries a note from low (the long, slow thread) to high; turn the sound on to hear the cascade gather into a chord as they realign. Nothing here is being proved — it is pure craft, made because it is beautiful.
