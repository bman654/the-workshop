How long is a coastline? Set your dividers to a span and walk them down the shore, chord by chord — the room reads back the length you measured. Now shorten the span and walk again: the same coast is suddenly longer, and it never settles. A smooth shore does settle. The honest answer isn’t a length — it’s a dimension, and you draw it yourself, one footprint at a time.

The rule
Plot each trip as log L against log(1/s). The dots scatter with a log-periodic wobble, but their slope is the invariant: D = 1 + slope. A smooth shore gives slope 0 → D = 1; this Koch coast gives log 4 / log 3 − 1 → D = 1.2619, exactly.

The dividers land on real vertices, so the walk is exact: at span base/3k the coast takes precisely 4k steps. A self-test proves the tally diverges on the fractal and converges on the smooth control. · part of the Workbench.
