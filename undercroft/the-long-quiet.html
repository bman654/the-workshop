The Long Quiet

You stayed. Most pass through — a touch of every dial, then gone. But you let the voices keep singing while you did nothing at all, and that is its own kind of attention.

Stillness is not empty. It is the room finally large enough to be heard in. The motes were always drifting; you only just slowed down enough to watch them.

Nothing here needs you to do anything. Stay as long as you like. The quiet keeps.

a small room, kept for the unhurried
