The Keeper of Tales

Most who pass through the Lantern light one tale, perhaps two, and move on. You walked all three to their last pages — and a tale, walked to its end, is a thing you carry the rest of the way home.

So the teller keeps this small alcove, lit and quiet, for the ones who finished. Sit a while. The lanterns you carried are here.

  • 🏮 The Lamplighter You called the dawn down, lamp by lamp, until the dark had nowhere left to stand.
  • 🏮 The Ferryman You paid the toll and made the crossing — and learned what is owed, and what is given.
  • 🏮 The Clockmaker You set the hour right, gear by patient gear, until the workshop kept true time again.

There is a fourth lantern the teller does not hang on the wall. It is lit only after closing, when the last visitor has gone and the keeper walks the dark round alone. You have found your way to it once already, perhaps — the night shift keeps its own hours.
