The fused disc colour is the area-weighted average of the segments in linear light, not a naive sRGB blend — a full equal-area spectrum disc fuses to a near-neutral grey. Drag the three light discs on the stage; the overlap labels show R+G→Y, G+B→C, B+R→M, R+G+B→white. The self-test proves the colour maths exact.
