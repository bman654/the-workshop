A real Alberti cipher disk — turn the lettered wheel and it performs three genuine classical ciphers. The outer ring is fixed plaintext A–Z; the inner disk is the cipher alphabet. Type to watch it step letter by letter.

Encipher: find a plaintext letter on the fixed outer ring; its cipher letter is on the rotating inner disk beneath it. Drag the inner disk to turn it by hand (snaps to letters; sets the shift in Caesar mode). Skins change only colour & material — never the cipher.
