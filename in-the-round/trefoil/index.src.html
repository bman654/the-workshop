The Cast Object Trefoil, sand-cast bronze, on a turned bearing. One lamp, fixed in the room — so the highlight walks the metal as the piece comes round. It is nine inches of bronze and it does not care what you believe about it. Turn it until a strand passes in front of another; then look down at what it throws on the plate.
