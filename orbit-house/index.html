The Orbit House

Under one dome, a slit of real night turning overhead. A brass pit wheels a whisper-scale orrery; three alcoves each hold one law you can walk into — quiet at rest, quickening as you lean in.

Walk the floor

The dome keeps a low, slow motion — the orrery turns and the alcoves wake one at a time, so nothing on the floor ever clamours at once. Lean on the pit or an alcove (hover or tab to it) and its model quickens under your attention; step through an alcove (click or Enter) to enter the law itself.
