What you are operating

This is not a chart of a swing ride — it is one, seen front-on. Six chairs hang on chains from a hub of radius r₀. When the carousel turns at rate ω, each chair flies out to a steady lean θ from vertical and rides a horizontal circle. At that balance the chain's vertical pull carries gravity and its horizontal pull is exactly the centripetal force the orbit needs: T cos θ = mg, T sin θ = mω²R. Divide and the chain tension cancels: tan θ = ω²R/g. The catch — and the whole reason this is interesting — is that R is not fixed: as the chair leans out it reaches farther from the axis, R = r₀ + L·sin θ, so θ sits on both sides. The lean is the root of that implicit equation, and the protractor needle is that root.

The crank — one honest verb

The brass crank (and its slider twin) sets ω and nothing else. As you turn it, the canopy opens like an umbrella: the chairs fly out (the orbit R widens) and rise (their drop L·cos θ shrinks). Both tells come from the same θ. The protractor on the side and the little arc on the fullest chair share one mapper — both read the same solveLean(ω). An engraved ghost tick marks where you were a moment ago, so re-cranking shows the lean climb past your last mark. Near the ceiling the chairs are flung nearly flat — θ approaches 90° but never reaches it for any finite spin.

The rigid-spoke control

Flip on rigid spokes and the chains become bolted I-beam arms. Now crank all you like: the lean stays pinned at 0° — locked arms cannot fly out, so the chairs just whirl flat at the rest radius r₀, no rise, no splay. That is the neg-control you can feel: chains open the umbrella; rigid spokes do nothing to the lean. The self-test proves solveLeanRigid(ω) ≡ 0 across every spin where a real swing leans — they agree only at ω = 0, where both honestly read zero.

Honesty

This is the equilibrium lean — the steady angle a swing settles to once it has flown out and stopped oscillating. The page eases the canopy toward that exact angle with a damped spring so it swings in like real chains; that approach transient is the only decorative part — every settled value (θ, R, rise) is the exact core value, and the live residual f(θ)≈0 on the ledger is the proof. The crank maps to a ceiling of ω = 3.5 rad/s so the ride never enters the numerically-harsh near-90° regime. Idealized: point-mass chairs, massless inextensible chains, no air drag.
