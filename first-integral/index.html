Why a bowl? The Beltrami identity says the true curve conserves a quantity H = f − y′·(∂f/∂y′) all along its arc — the gold strip stays flat. The deeper truth is that it minimises the whole integral, so the cost surface curves up in every direction: a true minimum, ΔI ∝ (pull)² for small pulls. The self‑test proves it exact — every sampled drag costs more, the cost quadruples when you pull twice as far, and the strip wavers past tolerance — while keeping H flat to machine precision on the true curve.
