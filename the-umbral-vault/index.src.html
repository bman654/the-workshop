The Umbral Vault

Six openings, and every one is only bright because the sun is falling through it.

drag the sun across the sky

One sun, six children. Drag it from dawn to dusk and the whole vault answers in a breath — the caustic warms, the mirage blooms and drains, the wash reddens, the bent ray re-breaks itself. Only the oculus keeps its own, older time. Each brass plaque is the door to the room its light came from. ← back to the Hall of Mirrors
