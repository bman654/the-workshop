The Soap Film

Dip two rings of wire in soapy water and a film spans the gap. Surface tension pulls it to the smallest possible area — and the only such shape is the catenoid, a hanging-chain curve spun around. Pull the rings apart and the film thins… until, past a sharp threshold, it can’t hold — it snaps into two flat discs. Drag the stage to rotate.
