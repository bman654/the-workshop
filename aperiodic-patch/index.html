What this proves. Filling this finite patch demonstrates local non-periodicity in exactly what you build — the repeat-hunter finds no translation that maps the patch onto itself — alongside a control tile-set that does repeat under the very same test. It is not a from-scratch proof that the Penrose rhombs admit no periodic tiling at all; that theorem (Penrose, de Bruijn) is deeper. The self-test pill re-runs the three legs the Node twin proves.
