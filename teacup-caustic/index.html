what you are looking at
The bright heart on your morning coffee is a real catacaustic — the envelope of the rays a lamp reflects off the cup's round inner wall. Each ray obeys the law of reflection; where neighbouring reflected rays pile up, the light is densest, and that pile-up is the curve.
The whole fact lives in the wall's curvature. A source exactly on the rim gives a cardioid — one cusp. Lift it off, out toward the sun, and a second cusp is born: the nephroid — two cusps. Flatten the wall and the rays just slide to one virtual image — no cusp, no caustic. The dial does nothing, which is the point.
reflected ray dir at wall-angle t  =  (R·cos2t − cos t,  R·sin2t − sin t). The caustic is its envelope; the loupe reads the cusps straight off it. An exact 2-D geometric catacaustic in cup-units — not a brightness render.
