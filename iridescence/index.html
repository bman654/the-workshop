The colours of soap bubbles, oil on a puddle, and Newton's rings — computed, not painted. Light reflecting off the two faces of a thin film interferes; the path difference 2nt cosθ′ cancels some wavelengths and reinforces others. Each pixel's colour is its reflected spectrum integrated against the CIE 1931 observer.
