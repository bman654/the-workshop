The Arctic Circle

Order has a coastline. Shuffle a perfectly random domino tiling of an Aztec diamond and the four corners can't help but freeze into a forced brick-wall — one orientation each, in solid colour — while the centre stays a salt-and-pepper churn of all four. The boundary between the frozen calm and the temperate storm is, as the diamond grows, a perfect circle: the disk inscribed in the diamond. Press GROW and watch it assemble; drag the SIZE dial and watch the coastline sharpen.

Each domino is coloured by its orientation. In the frozen corners every tile is one colour — they're forced, no choice left. Only inside the circle do all four colours mix: that churn is the temperate region, and it fills exactly π/4 of the diamond as n → ∞.
