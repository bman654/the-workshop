The claim, self-tested

Four proven facts, integers only:

1 · Valid iff every district is one orthogonally-contiguous blob AND equal population: base = ⌊N/K⌋, the +1 remainder to the LOWEST district ids.
2 · Winner = majority; tie → RED. Threshold T = ⌊pop/2⌋+1. A winner wastes votes above T; a loser wastes all of its.
3 · EG = (wastedR − wastedB) / N, a gcd-reduced fraction — and it equals (drainedred − drainedblue) pips on the board exactly.
4 · COMPACT proves the minimum |EG| over every valid map of the same voters — the gap was the drawing.
Why a district line. A redistricting bench — the Pavilion's first civic puzzle and its first carve verb. You never change a vote; you only draw the lines. A packed landslide drains to a grey block; a cracked loser drains wholly — and the imbalance of drained pips is the efficiency gap you read before any needle moves. PACK and CRACK are two distinct abuses that both rob the majority; COMPACT collapses the gap to its proven minimum on the identical electorate.
