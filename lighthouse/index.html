optical bench — Fresnel lens

A thick converging lens bends parallel light to a focus — but most of the glass only adds bulk. Slice the lens into concentric zones, keep only the curved skin of each, and collapse them onto a flat sheet: the Fresnel lens. Same focus F, a fraction of the weight — the innovation that let a lighthouse throw a beam for miles.
