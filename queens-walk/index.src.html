The Queen's Long Walk

the safe squares are the golden ratio
checking…

One queen on a board. She moves like a chess queen but in one quadrant only — left, down, or diagonally down-left, any distance. Land her on the home corner and you win. A flint opponent answers with provably perfect play, read straight from a fully-solved table. Click a square along one of her rays to slide her there. Lose twice and the board reveals its secret: the squares you keep losing from light up — and they fall on two golden lines.

cold square — hand it over & you win warm — a winning slide exists
The cold squares are (⌊nφ⌋, ⌊nφ²⌋). The golden ratio, hidden in a game of slides.
Every verdict is read from the same proven table the green chip verifies — the page and the headless proof share one solver. Reveal the rails and the cold squares light along two lines climbing at the golden ratio φ. They are the only squares from which every slide hands the opponent a winning board — so the whole game is: shove the queen onto the next cold square and never let go. Turn the diagonal ray off and the rails vanish — the cold squares collapse onto the main diagonal (plain two-heap Nim), and φ disappears.
