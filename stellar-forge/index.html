Where a star is weighed, not just watched. A star spends its whole life in a standoff — gravity pulling inward forever, fusion pushing back — and when the fuel runs out, the only thing left holding the core up is quantum degeneracy pressure. But that refusal has a ceiling. The Forge lets you put a dying core on the scales and add mass until the floor gives way. The body answers in light: it cools, it implodes, or it disappears. The companion bench works the other end of a star's life — the fusion ladder that builds the elements up to iron and then refuses to climb further.

⛓ The Fusion Ladder bench · next growth

A literal vertical ladder of rungs you ignite — H → He → C → … → Si → Fe. Each rung releases binding energy as long as you climb toward iron; at the cold-iron rung the ladder refuses to burn further, because binding-energy-per-nucleon peaks at the iron group (Fe-56 / Ni-62, ≈8.79 MeV/nucleon) and net fusion energy flips sign. The same iron peak that ends a massive star's life and lights the implosion the Scales begins. Sown for next growth.
