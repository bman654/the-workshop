The Meshing Wheels

the Chinese Remainder Theorem · cranked by hand

Crank the small wheel — drag its handle, press ⟳, or use ←/→. Every click, one new residue-pair passes the window.

Two brass wheels of coprime teeth mesh on one line. Each carries a chalk-marked tooth; crank, and the marks realign only every m·n turns. In between, every reading (x mod m, x mod n) passes the window exactly once — the residue pair is a bijection. Set two clock-readings below and the lone tooth that reads both at once is the unique x mod mn, reconstructed by hand. Swap to non-coprime wheels and watch the bijection break.
