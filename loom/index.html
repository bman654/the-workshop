A floor loom you can poke. Set the threading (top), tie-up (corner), and treadling (right) and the cloth re-weaves live by the loom equation. Drag to paint runs.

Threading — top strip; click a column to step its shaft, drag to paint.
Tie-up — corner block; click to toggle a lift.
Treadling — right strip; click a row to step its treadle, drag to paint.
Warp / weft swatches — the thin coloured edges; click to step yarn colour, drag to paint runs (try log-cabin).
Hover the cloth to see the loom equation light up its threading end, treadling pick & tie-up cell.
