The Measuring Bench

The greatest common divisor is not a chart of remainders — it is a thing your hands do. Lay the short rod against the long one and cut off its length, again and again, racing down to where one rod measures the other exactly. That last rod is the GCD — and you can lay it across both originals to watch it tile them with no gap left over.
