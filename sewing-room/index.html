The Sewing Room

A whole room given to one loop of string — the simplest 1-D thing there is, and the things you may legally do to it. Wrap it round itself and it can become genuinely knotted — a tangle no amount of wiggling will undo; lift it through your fingers in the right order and it forms a cat's cradle — figures that exist only as a sequence of legal moves; shake a loose strand and watch a knot get earned. Four benches; four ways a loop of string keeps its own kind of law — and each is honest about exactly what it proves.

The Knot Tabulator
knot theory · the determinant |Δ(−1)|

When is a tangled loop truly knotted, and not just a mess that falls apart when you tug it? Encode a diagram as a signed Gauss code, compute its knot determinant, then wiggle it with random Reidemeister moves: under every legal move the number cannot budge. Trefoil 3, figure-8 5, unknot 1 — a number that proves a loop is knotted.

The Cat's-Cradle Weaver
string figures · a formal move-grammar

A cat's cradle is not a drawing — it is a sequence of legal finger-pickups. Lift the loop through the right fingers and the next figure (Opening → Cradle → Soldier's Bed → Candles → Manger) forms; reach for a pickup the rules forbid and nothing happens — the string does not move. Watch each figure weave itself from the real string, then try a forbidden move and feel it be rejected.

The Unknotting Bench
knot theory · the untying verb & the |Δ|=1 obstruction

The Tabulator proved a number can't budge; now use your hands. Tap a crossing, pick a legal verb (untwist · unpoke · slide), and watch the real loop simplify in place. A disguised loop unties down a ladder to a bare circle — while the trefoil refuses: every verb goes dead, because |Δ|=3 can never become 1. You can untie a disguise; you cannot untie a theorem.

The Tangle Bench
topology · entropy · the knot determinant

Where does a knot come from? Hold SHAKE and a loose strand writhes like a dropped garden hose — and a live detector reads the wing's own |Δ(−1)| straight off the geometry, the same number the Tabulator computes another way. Lengthen the rope, shake harder, and a loop earns a knot; PULL-TIGHT locks a real knot but only relaxes a disguise. Agitation earns a knot; a number reads it off the writhe.
