what this proves, and what it doesn't

  • One law, two costumes. The tank's ω (read from the lc-tank core's own omega) is 1/√(LC); the pendulum's ω₀ (read from the swing-ship core's own omega0) is √(g/L). Set L_pend = g·L_lc·C and the two are equal to < 1e-9 over the whole L×C sweep (worst ~4.4e-16) — the g cancels, no fudge.
  • The SAME function of time. Boot both at rest at an extremum and integrate a full period with each core's own RK4; after normalizing (q̂ = q/Q0, î = i/(ωQ0); θ̂ = θ/θ0, θ̇̂ = θ̇/(ωθ0)) the two states agree to < 1e-9 (measured ~4.9e-15). That single shared stroke IS the ribbon.
  • The phase map is pinned. Both boot as pure cosines from rest, so q↔θ (both peak together — bob at arc end) and i = q̇ ↔ θ̇ (both zero-cross together — coil flare = bob through bottom-dead-centre). The lockstep is not eyeballed; it is the same cosine.
  • The negative control bleeds (touchable). Throw the damp lever and the bob's energy is strictly monotone-down (read from the SAME oracle the badge reads — never a cosmetic decay): after 24 cycles E/E0 ≈ 3e-11, and its arc diverges from its free twin by ~0.22 rad. The conserved-energy classifier returns free ✓ for both free legs and ✗ for the bled one — a vacuous always-pass fails.
  • Idealized, exact law. The tank is the lc-tank bench's lossless LC (the RLC term is the neg-control); the pendulum is the small-angle linear leg of the swing-ship (NOT its Mathieu pump or nonlinear sinθ — those are a different bench). The two ω's are exact; only the puppet animation is eased for the eye. The aesthetic constants are not proven; the equivalence is.
