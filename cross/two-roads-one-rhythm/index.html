Two Roads, One Rhythm

two humps fork at the same shrinking instants

One brass dial, the ladder depth d — an integer, 0 → 8. Turn it and two roads that live on different r-axes fork in lockstep: the logistic hump x → r·x(1−x) on top, a sine hump x → r·sin(πx) below. Each detent splits every rung in two (1→2→4→8→16), and both roads fork on the same detent. Their rung values differ (the where is not shared); their fork instants shrink by the same ratio — Feigenbaum's δ → 4.6692016…. That shared when is universality.

Honest scope. The dial is the rung index, not a raw r — the only coordinate both roads honestly share (they live on [0,4] vs [0,1]). The δ meters read a live-measured ratio off each road's superstable ladder; at finite depth they sit a hair off the limit and crawl toward it. Universality is conditional on a smooth quadratic maximum — flip the second road to the tent (a corner, no curve) and it climbs one rung then refuses to fork: no cascade, δ undefined.

what is exact, and what is an estimate

  • The shared rhythm is real. Both roads are run by the same lifted engine (one superstable-ladder solver), fed two different humps. At depth ≥6 the two measured ratios agree to < 0.01 (worst 8.48e-4) and both sit on δ = 4.669201609…. That sameness — same when, different where — is universality.
  • The δ meter is a live estimate, not a hardcoded constant. It reads the ratio of the road's last two shrinking windows. At small depth it sits a hair off the limit and crawls toward it as you deepen the dial — a limit, not luck.
  • The rung values differ; that is the point. The logistic 2-cycle superstable point is R₁ = 1+√5 = 3.2360680; the sine road's is 0.7777338. Wildly different values, one ratio limit. Universality is not a coincidence of values.
  • It is conditional on a smooth maximum. Flip to tent (piecewise-linear, a corner not a curve): the same engine climbs one rung and then can never fork — rungs read 9 / 9 / 1 at depth 8, and the tent's δ is undefined (the ratio set is empty — never a number). A vacuous "every hump gives 4.669" checker fails the tent.
