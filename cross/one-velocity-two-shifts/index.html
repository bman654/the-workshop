One Velocity, Two Shifts

a siren and a star share 1 + v/c

One gold groove — the radial speed fraction β = vradial/c. Drag the buzzing source along its track up top and two worlds that never met shift by the identical first-order factor: the siren's heard pitch rises by 1 + v/c, and the star's whole hydrogen comb slides rigid by the same λobs/λrest = 1 + v/c. When the two jewels coincide on the groove, a gold link draws — a siren's pitch-up and a star's blueshift are one fraction.

Honest scope. β is capped at ±0.05 (keeps the comb in-band; the classical headline stays faithful). The agreement is a first-order truth: the siren's exact factor 1/(1−β) and the star's 1+β diverge as an O(β²) term — the "teeth" — so the latch is a limit the exact forms outgrow, not a tautology. Two load-bearing controls make the seam visible: flip who-moves (sound is asymmetric, light is not) and tilt the velocity transverse (the acoustic shift vanishes to exactly 1 while relativity keeps γ).

what is exact, and what is an estimate

  • The shared fact is exact. The siren's first-order factor acousticFO(β) = 1+β and the star's classical factor spectralFactor(β) = 1+β (read through the comb's own shiftedNm) agree to < 1e-9 across the whole corridor (worst 1.11e-16), and spectralFactor is λ-independent — the same on every Balmer line (the rigid slide). The two cores share no code.
  • The agreement is first-order, not a tautology. The siren's exact factor 1/(1−β) departs the shared 1+β as an O(β²) term — the teeth — growing from ~9e-10 at β=3e-5 to ~2.6e-3 at the β=0.05 edge. The first-order latch is a first-order truth the exact forms outgrow.
  • Sound rides a medium; light does not. Flip who moves: the acoustic factor jumps from source 1/(1−β) to listener 1+β (a gap that grows 1.0e-6 → 1.01e-4), while spectralFactor is the same form regardless. A "symmetric in who-moves" classifier passes light / provably fails sound.
  • Transverse kills the acoustic shift but not γ. Tilt θ→90° (v_radial→0): the classical acoustic factor collapses to exactly 1 (rings re-centre) while the relativistic transverse factor keeps the Lorentz γ = 1/√(1−β²) (residual 4.5e-4 at β=0.03). A "purely-radial / always-agree" classifier fails it.
