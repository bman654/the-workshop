what this proves, and what it doesn't

  • One law, two readings. The model's split log-odds (read from the clockwork core's own softmax) is ln(p_the/p_on) = GAP/T, exact to < 2.2e-16. The ratchet's hop-bias log-odds (read from the engine room's own symmetricRates) is ln(r_fwd/r_bwd) = (−2τ)/T, exact to the same precision. Both are the exponential e−E/T, dead linear in 1/T.
  • The merge is honest — it is the tuning, not a fudge. Both lines pivot through the origin (T→∞ ⇒ 50/50), so the only freedom is the slope. The mind's is GAP = 1.3; the gas's is −2τ. Set −2τ = GAP (τ = −0.65) and they coincide to <1e-9 for every T. Mistune it and the gap |Δ| = |GAP − (−2τ)|/T GROWS with 1/T — the rays genuinely fan apart, so coincidence is the tuning, never a tautology.
  • It is not the apparatus — a negative control bites. Throw the knife-switch and softmax's exp is swapped for a linear mix p_i ∝ (z_i/T + c). Its log-odds is no longer linear in 1/T — the marker BENDS off the straight ray — and no τ can align a bent curve with a straight one. It is the exponential shared by softmax and Arrhenius that makes T the same dial; a vacuous "always linear" checker provably fails the bent curve.
  • Toy, exact law. The model is the clockwork bench's frozen toy (|V|=8 tokens, one logit vector); the ratchet is the engine room's reduced dimensionless rate model. The two log-odds are exact; only the wheel's idle spin and the bead's throw are eased for the eye (the wheel render is history-free — a picture of the current rate, never an accumulating odometer).
