The Fold They Share

a strobed wheel and a moiré fringe share one fold

One brass dial — the spoke rate of a spinning wheel, swept past a fixed strobe. Turn it and two phenomena that never met fold down to the identical slow rate. The strobed wheel aliases in TIME: its apparent crawl is |apparentRate|. The two gratings beat in SPACE: their fat moiré band has the reciprocal-beat magnitude 1/D = |1/p₁ − 1/p₂|. Both are the same difference of reciprocals — and the gold ruler down the middle drops both magnitudes on the same mark. A chip latches when they coincide to < 1e-9.

What's exact, what's drama. The shared number is the magnitude; the wheel's sign is the drama — below the strobe it crawls backward (the movie phantom), at coincidence it freezes, above it crawls forward. The latch holds inside the fundamental band (|spokeRate − strobe| ≤ strobe/2), where the eye folds against the first strobe multiple. The grating pitches are drawn at a badged pixel scale (the periods are tiny) — but the ruler always reads the true reciprocals. The load-bearing control: set spoke rate === strobe and the fold vanishes both ways — the wheel stands dead still and the moiré field goes flat (D→∞). No beat is the correct answer.

what is exact, and what is drama

  • The shared fold is exact. Inside the fundamental band the wheel's apparent magnitude |apparentRate(spoke, strobe)| and the gratings' reciprocal-beat 1/spacingTwoPitch(1/spoke, 1/strobe) agree to < 1e-9 across a dense sweep (worst ~7e-15). Both equal |spoke − strobe| = |1/T_w − 1/T_s| — the same difference of reciprocals. The two cores are lifted byte-for-byte and share no code.
  • The sign is the wagon-wheel reversal, not a tautology. The reciprocal-beat is unsigned, but apparentRate carries a sign — negative just below the strobe (the wheel runs backward, the classic movie phantom), positive just above, exactly zero at coincidence. The magnitude still matches; the sign is real motion.
  • The coincidence control is load-bearing. Set spoke === strobe (p₁ === p₂) and the fold goes to zero on both sides at once: apparentRate === 0 (the wheel freezes) AND spacingTwoPitch === Infinity (the field goes flat, D→∞). A vacuous "two combs always beat / a strobed wheel always drifts" classifier passes every off-coincidence point and provably fails here — the absence of a beat is the right answer, reported as a clean limit (never NaN).
  • The pitches are drawn at a scale; the math is not. The wheel's and strobe's true periods are tiny fractions of a second, so the gratings are drawn at a badged pixel scale to keep the band visible. The ruler and every readout use the true reciprocals — the scale never touches the latch.
