The Gnomon

a sundial that tells true clock time
checking…
set your place & the date — drag the dial to move time

A real instrument, not a picture of one. The style (the gnomon's edge) is cut to your latitude so it points at the celestial pole; its shadow falls on hour-lines laid out in closed form for the dial type. Read off apparent solar time, then add the longitude offset, the equation of time, and DST — and the dial agrees with your wall clock to the minute.

Drag the dial left/right to move the clock. The bright wedge is the cast shadow; its tip rides the numbered hour-line. Toggle the equation of time and watch the civil readout snap onto the wall clock. Plot analemma traces a year of true noons — the figure-8.
