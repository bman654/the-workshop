push-only · a crate never comes back

Every other puzzle in this wing forgives you — fill a wrong cell, draw a wrong edge, place a wrong bulb, and you just erase it. Here you are a porter who can only PUSH. Shove a crate into a corner and it stays there forever: the room can become unwinnable while every move you made was legal. This is the logic puzzle where the solver can LOSE.
