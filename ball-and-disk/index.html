The Reckoning Cabinet · the wheel that integrates

Drag the brass ball along the track. Its distance from the centre is the radius x. The disc spins by itself through angle u; a ball that rolls without slipping turns the output wheel by dθ = (x·du)/r. So the odometer dial winds the running integral θ = (1/r) ∫x du — no arithmetic anywhere. The gold trail is the arc the ball inks on the disc as it rolls; the odometer counts how much of that arc has passed under the ball.

Honest scope: this is the ideal no-slip point-contact law — the governing relation θ = (1/r)∫x du, not a friction model. A real ball creeps a little; slip scales the output by a constant but never changes the law's form. We assert the law, not the tribology.
