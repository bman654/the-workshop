The Shadow Theater

Drag the paper — dolly the lamp — let the crane take flight
Drag a shadow to slide it · drag its rod up/down to push it toward the lamp (it looms and softens) or back to the silk (crisp and small) · drag the lamp's glow to dolly the whole scene · sweep the crane's wing to send it up across the moon. The leaf keeps a cast to the shelf.
