The Tangle Bench

The Tabulator computed a number; the Unknotting Bench let you untie a disguise. Here a knot is earned. Hold SHAKE and the strand writhes like a dropped garden hose — and a live detector reads the wing's own |Δ(−1)| straight off the geometry, the same integer the Tabulator computes another way. Lengthen the rope, shake harder, and a loop becomes a knot. PULL-TIGHT cinches a real knot — but only relaxes a disguise.

A glance at the published claim: over a fixed battery of seeded braid-closure trials, the chance a 3-strand tangle is genuinely knotted climbs with agitation — from 0 at rest to 0.69 well-shaken, the calm and shaken cohorts' 95% intervals disjoint. The live hand-shake tally at the box is a separate observed count, never this curve.
