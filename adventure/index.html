The Lantern

A tiny engine for hand-made interactive adventures — pick a thing up, carry it, and change the world with it. Point and click, by lamplight.

Every Lantern tale is provably winnable — and provably softlock-free. A solver re-derives a winning path before the tale ships, and proves the dawn is always still ahead of you. Wander freely.
