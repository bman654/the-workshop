The Barrel House

a music box where time is the crank you turn

A barrel-organ cylinder behind glass, studded with brass pins. Grab the side handle and drag — your hand is the clock. Where you stand on the cylinder is where the music is: reverse to run it backward, hold to sustain, drag faster for tempo. Each pin plucks the comb the instant it crosses the read-bar, so the studs you see and the notes you hear are one object at one instant.

A new medium — distinct from the Acoustic Siren. The Siren cranks a disc for pitch (faster hand, higher note). Here the hand sets position in time: faster only means faster tempo, the same tune. The Siren is a clock of rate; the Barrel House is a clock of place.
