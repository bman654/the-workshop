[low voice] One more thing, and then I'll open the doors. [short pause] The estate keeps secrets. Locked rooms. Hidden trails. Things that open only for visitors who notice. [pause] I won't show you any of them — a secret shown from a stage stops being one. But I will tell you they are there, and that they are fair: everything hidden can be found, and everything findable has a way in. [short pause] Some of you will go looking. [pause] Good.
