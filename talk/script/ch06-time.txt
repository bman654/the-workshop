Time gets a whole wing. [short pause] The orrery runs on Kepler's clock — the inner planets whirl, the outer ones stroll, a day of theirs in a second of yours.

[pause] And underground, the simplest clock that can exist: one photon, two mirrors, tick. Set it moving and the path slants — same speed of light, longer path, slower tick. That slant is Pythagoras. The whole of special relativity, hiding in a right triangle. [short pause] I keep both because they are the same subject at two altitudes: the sky keeping time for everyone, and geometry quietly explaining why nobody's watch agrees.
