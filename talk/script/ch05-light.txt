[pause] And this is the far end of the same story. Two slits, one particle at a time. Each arrives as a single dot — yet the dots pile up into interference fringes. [short pause] I fired those just now, while you watched. [pause] Light will not tell you what it is. I find that endlessly companionable — an estate full of proofs, anchored by a thing that refuses to be settled.
