Chomp. Tap a square to eat it and every square up-and-right of it. The top-left square is ☠ poison — force your opponent to eat it and you win. You move first; the house plays a provably perfect reply.

The gap you can feel. Strategy-stealing proves the first player wins every non-1×1 bar: whatever winning reply the opponent has to the top-right corner bite, the first player could have just opened with it — so a winning first move must exist. The proof names a winner — and never a winning move. (On this bar the corner bite itself is usually a losing opening: solve() knows the win exists, but the very lever the proof leans on is not the move.)

☠ The lone exception is the 1×1 bar: only poison, so the first player must eat it. The single board strategy-stealing cannot cover — its lamp stays dark.
