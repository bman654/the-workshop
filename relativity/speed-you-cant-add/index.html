Switch off relativity

instrument bench · the falsifier

Throw the switch with both throttles past half: the ball blows through the red c-line — super-luminal, impossible. The ruler's two segments overshoot its end: lengths no longer compose. Linear addition is visibly the wrong law. The chip top-right proves it live.

The ball's lab speed is relativistic velocity addition, w = (u+v)/(1+uv); it equals tanh(atanh u + atanh v) — velocities compose because rapidities add. Two sub-c speeds never reach c. The same throttle the Twin Voyage flies; the same sky the Starbow bends.
