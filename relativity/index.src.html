The shape of the gap

Below the rail your speed draws a ribbon; under it, the shaded band is √(1−β²) — how fast your clock runs at each instant. The band's area is your τ; the gap to the ceiling is the time you'll never get back.

Store & compare two voyages

Fly a voyage, then store it as A; fly a different shape and store B — the verdict says which shape aged you less, and by how much. (Same home-clock window; only the shape of β(t) differs.)

Your τ is the integral τ = ∫√(1−β(t)²) dt over the whole flight. At constant β it lands on the closed form t/γ; and any detour — longer or faster — always costs strictly more.
