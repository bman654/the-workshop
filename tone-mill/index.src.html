A fixed strobe-edge hears each tooth pass — that is the whole instrument: the tone is the rate teeth go by, f = N·Ω/2π. Two concentric rings on the SAME Ω sound a just interval and freeze as one chord; detune one and the moiré crawls and the chord sours. Hit detach the needle and the pitch comes from a separate knob: the strobe still freezes the disc but the heard Hz drifts off N·Ω/2π — proof it's the shared phase, not two tuned dials, that makes seen & heard one thing.
