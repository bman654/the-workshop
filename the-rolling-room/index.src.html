The Rolling Room

A thin layer of fluid, heated from below. Turn the heat up gently and nothing happens — it just sits there and conducts, dead still. But cross one exact threshold and the whole layer spontaneously breaks into a row of slow, counter-rotating rolls. That threshold is the critical Rayleigh number, Rac = 27π⁴/4 = 657.5113645 — and the rolls you watch are the honest fluid solver, not an animation. Drive the heat the wrong way (warm on top) and it can never start. Drag the dial up across the notch, and drop a bead of dye to watch it get seized.
