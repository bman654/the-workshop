Drag the drum to spin it — it coasts and slows on its own friction. Look through the near slits to the far wall: only through a moving slit do the stills lock into motion.
The slit is the shutter. Between slits the picture is hidden — your eye stitches the flashes into a single moving thing. The Victorians called it the wheel of life.
