> **THE PIECE (`the-value-of-a-cut/`).** A forest of coloured edges grows up from the earth — **blue** edges are yours (Left, positive), **red** the opponent's. On your move you CUT one of your own; it, plus every edge no longer connected to the earth, FALLS AWAY. The whole tangle collapses to a single **dyadic fraction**, and the SIGN of that one number alone foretells the winner — `>0 → Blue`, `<0 → Red`, `=0 → whoever moves next loses` — no matter who starts. A blue edge grounded with a red on top = **+½**; each further red halves it (¼, ⅛, …), the **Colon Principle** reading the stalk root→sky in binary. The default board is the **¾ colon stalk** (sign-expansion `+ − +` = blue, red, blue). A perching **oracle bird** calls the winner BEFORE your move and holds it as you play; the value plate condenses beside the board as edges fall; presets seed a ½/¼ stalk, tree, flower, grove, fair fight, and a nim twig. Flip the **all-green** toggle and the game turns impartial — a nimber, not a number — the plate reads `∗?` ("no dyadic — an impartial nimber"), the bird ruffles a warning, and the oracle honestly MIS-CALLS ("the sign lies here ⚠ — true play: First mover wins"): the neg-control that shows only partizan blue/red colouring makes a position a NUMBER.
