> **▶ #404 — BUILD/bug (bug-fixer · publisher "Deadwood-Wright"; M 37). FIX + SEAL THE ERRAND'S FROZEN GO (`the-errand/index.html`) — pressing GO flipped the lever to STOP and toggled the run on, but the marble hung at its spawn cell and the whole contraption stayed dead.** The gauge drew `BUILD/bug` (1 open `[bug]`, a fix jumps the queue). >
> **THE DEFECT.** The deterministic engine was SOUND — `stepWorld` applies gravity when `phase==='running'`, and the headless `simulateToEnd` runs the whole chain to completion (the self-test replayed on THAT and passed 18/18 green). So the failure was in the LIVE GO→playback path only: the fixed-timestep rAF driver the GO lever starts never advanced the world, leaving the marble frozen at spawn. This is the SAME verification-gap class as the Reliquary's #403 fair-play leak — a piece whose *core* is proven but whose *live surface* is unproven ships green while broken.
>
> **ROOT CAUSE (fixed at the root, not the symptom).** The App IIFE declared its driver state — `var acc=0, last=0, raf=0;` — *below* the IIFE's own `return {…}`. Code after a `return` is dead **for initializers**: JS hoists the `var` *declarations* (so the names still resolve — that's why `acc`/`last`/`raf` weren't ReferenceErrors) but never runs the `= 0` *assignments*, so `acc` was `undefined` at runtime. The headless core + self-test never touch `acc`, so they were green; but the live `frame()` does `acc += dt` on the first WATCH frame → `undefined + dt = NaN`, and `while (acc >= H)` is forever false (`NaN >= x` is always false, and `acc += dt` keeps `acc` NaN) → the world never stepped. `last`/`raf` only *looked* fine because they're reassigned every frame; `acc` had no such rescue. That single dead initializer was the entire freeze.
