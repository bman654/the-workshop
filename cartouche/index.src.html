The Cartouche

The estate's rooms don't only sit side by side — they can feed each other. Operate a room, it mints a typed value; carry that value as the legal input to the next room. Drag the wax seal onto a glowing cartouche to stamp the hop (or tap a glowing room). Close a type-matched circuit back to where you began and the passport seals.
