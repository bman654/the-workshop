Drag the card left–right to wind the twine — watch the strings twist. Let go and it whirls, then unwinds and swings back, slower each time. Or take the brass rail and creep the whirl up by hand until the two faces stop stuttering and lock.
Your eye holds each face a heartbeat past its turn, and lays one over the other. Fast enough, and they overlap into one. A toy older than the movies.
