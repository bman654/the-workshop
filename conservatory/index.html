🧬the gene jar

The Gene Jar

Pour two colours of bead — a fraction p gold (A), the rest slate (a) — crank once at random, and the three genotype towers settle on p² : 2pq : q² and stay there. The pool forgets every generation yet keeps its shape: one crank of random mating is equilibrium, held forever. Flip the assortative-mating knife-switch and the heterozygotes drain — but p never moves.

p²+2pq+q²=1 to the bit for any p; p a true fixed point (p′=p to <1e−9) reached in ONE step; an honest √N scoop fit; and a neg-control — assortative mating drains Aa with p UNCHANGED, flipping the identity RED.

🧠differential growth

The Wrinkling

A different kind of living thing under the glass: not a population that oscillates, but a single closed thread that simply keeps growing where it has no room — and so it folds, into brain-coral, leaf-curl, the frill of a kelp blade. One rule (grow · can’t-overlap · bounded room) buckles a slack circle into every organic edge. Turn the crowding dial and the same organism reorganizes under your hand; drop a stone and the sheet drapes past it.

pure generative art — it makes no claim but one honest invariant: a simple closed curve that never crosses itself, a live conscience pill that should never go red, re-proven by a Node twin over thousands of frames. Arc-length climbs past ×7 the room’s fixed perimeter.

🪲selection on a visible trait

The Selection Jar

A jar of beetles you can SEE, on a background you tint. A predator eats the most-visible each generation; survivors breed offspring a shade like their parents — and the cloud crawls until it vanishes into the colour you chose. The Gene Jar keeps its shape; this jar remembers which way the light came from — camouflage you watch evolve, not an allele table.

the per-generation mean-shift tracks the breeder’s equation R = h²·S on the ensemble mean (within tol); h²=0 ⇒ no tracking despite identical culling; selection OFF ⇒ drift only.

🎲pure genetic drift

The Drift Jar

The third jar, and the purest: a finite pool of p gold beads, the rest slate. Nothing selects, nothing mutates — every generation each bead just copies a random parent. RUN it and the colours wander by sheer copying-luck until one is gone forever. A finite pool always drifts and always ends monochrome — the Gene Jar next door is its N→∞ limit, where the drift switches off.

on the dense (2N+1)-state Wright–Fisher matrix the fixation probability = the initial frequency (absorption uᵢ=i/M to the bit); the ensemble mean never drifts (a martingale); the heterozygosity drains by exactly 1−1/2N per gen; N→∞ ⇒ pinned, never fixes.

🏠emergent segregation

Schelling’s Tipping Town

The Drift Jar’s kin, but the tipping is no longer luck — it is a wish. Every resident is content the moment even a third of its neighbours match, and would gladly share a mixed street. Yet drag the one tolerance slider, press play, and a random salt-and-pepper of gold and slate avalanches into stark blocks — a segregation near three-quarters, more than twice the mild wish that caused it. Pull the wish to zero and the starkness vanishes: it was never in the colours.

at τ=0.33 every seeded run settles segregation >0.70 (measured ≈0.75); neg-control τ=0 ⇒ zero moves & index≈½; the satisfied-swap like-pair potential Φ is non-decreasing and bounded by E=4WH−3W−3H+2 ⇒ guaranteed halt. Random-hop dynamics labeled modeled.
