The Pond

a school of fish · one harvest dial · the edge you can over-net
core checking…

A brass-edged tank of swimming fish breeding toward the carrying capacity K. Turn the one harvest dial and you start pulling fish out at a steady rate — the school thins or thrives in real time. Net gently and it holds a steady catch forever; net at the sweet spot and the bucket fills fastest of all; net past the edge and the pond goes empty — and stays empty. The dial can never undo that. The count you SEE, the catch you BANK, and the proof are one and the same fish.

What's proven vs what you're watching. The deterministic core proves the saddle-node exact: the two rest counts collide at N = K/2 = 50 at the critical harvest h_crit = rK/4 = 15 (= the maximum sustainable yield), and for any larger harvest the rest vanishes — the pond drains to 0 from any start and stays there. The living school illustrates that proof; it does not claim the fish-tank proves the continuous fold. The proof lives in the green self-test pill and in core.test.mjs.

The harvested logistic: N' = r·N·(1−N/K) − h, the colony from next door with a constant catch h drained off it. r=0.6, K=100, h are illustrative teaching landmarks (inherited from the logistic bench so the two never drift) — not a real fishery. What is exact is the bifurcation structure.
