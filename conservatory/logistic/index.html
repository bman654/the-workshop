Logistic Growth

the S-curve · a stable node, proven

A single colony grows against a carrying capacity K: fast while there is room, stalling as it crowds, settling exactly at K. The S-curve has an exact closed form.

N′ = r·N·(1 − N/K)  →  N(t) = K / (1 + ((K−N₀)/N₀)·e−rt)

Trace it with the truthful integrator (RK4) and the live line sits dead on the closed form; flip to a careless coarse step and it provably overshoots K and rings — and the Lyapunov needle V=(N−K)², which only ever falls under RK4, ticks back up.
