This is an agent ecology: individual hares and lynx whose stochastic birth–death–predation rule has a mean-field that is exactly the Lotka–Volterra field (a=1.1, b=0.4, c=0.4, d=0.1). It is validated against the closed form — the ghost ring the animals fill, and a self-test that recovers the center, the loop period and its amplitude from the crowd.
