The Midway

Physics you ride, not chart. Every ride here is a real instrument you operate and a body you sit inside; the readout is the motion itself. Six rides are running: shape a coaster and dare its loop over the top, drop a sealed cabin and watch the scale forget your weight, crank a swing carousel and read the lean its chains fly out to, spin a drum and drop the floor and stick to the wall, crank two spins of the teacups and bloom a flower, or pump your own length on the swing-ship and climb to the loop without a push. Nothing on this strip is a graph of an experience — it is the experience, run on honest physics.

The Coaster
the loop you dare to shape · energy & the 2.5r law

Shape the rail, set the loop's radius, pull the hoist lever — and the bead runs your track under gravity. It either whips over the top or peels off the rail and falls. The split is one number, and it is geometry: a loop survives only when you drop from at least 2½ loop-radii up. Watch the energy slosh, watch the rail-force needle peg to zero the instant the car detaches.

self-test ✓ · energy conserved <1e-9 · loop survives ⟺ h≥2.5r · sub-height car PROVABLY detaches

The Rotor
the floor drops, you stick to the wall · ωc = √(g/μr), mass-invariant

Step into a spinning drum, crank it up, and pull DROP THE FLOOR — the hinged plate swings down over a dark shaft. Above the threshold spin friction pins you to the wall; below it you sink in. A 95 kg adult and a 22 kg child share one fate: the crossing ωc = √(g/μr) has no mass in it, so they stick or sink the same. Flip the wall frictionless and it still presses but never holds — ωc runs off to ∞.

self-test ✓ · ωc=√(g/μr) MASS-INVARIANT · frictionless wall PROVABLY always slides → 🛗
The Drop Tower
the room fell with you, so the scale reads nothing · apparent weight N=m(g+a)

Hoist a sealed cabin up the mast and drop it — and the camera rides with you: the room holds still while the world streaks up past the windows. A loose coin lifts off the floor and hangs; the brass scale-needle pegs to zero. The room fell with you, so the scale reads nothing — until the brake slams it to the crush. Two spatial knobs trade drop height against the brake band; the crush is one exact, monotone number: peak g = h/d + 1.

self-test ✓ · N=0 across the whole fall · peak g = v²/(2d)+g · alwaysHeavy PROVABLY fails → 🎠
The Star Flyer
the lean your chains fly out to IS the spin · tan θ = ω²R/g

Crank the swing carousel up to speed and the chains fly out: the canopy opens like an umbrella as the chairs splay and rise. A brass protractor on the fullest chair reads the lean live — the needle is θ = solveLean(ω), the root of tan θ = ω²R/g with the orbit R = r₀ + L·sin θ widening as you go. Flip on rigid spokes and the same crank does nothing to the lean — locked arms whirl flat at θ = 0.

self-test ✓ · tan θ = ω²R/g (R=r₀+L sinθ, fixed point) · θ↑ in ω · rigid-spoke θ≡0 PROVABLY → 🍵
The Teacups
two spins make a flower · petals = the numerator of ω/Ω

Crank two spins by hand — drag the outer platter for Ω, the inner cup for ω — and the seat traces z(t) = R·e^{iΩt} + ρ·e^{i(Ω+ω)t}, a rose that closes only when ω/Ω is rational and grows one petal per numerator. The G-needle pins at the lurch and floats at the crown; the tea sloshes toward the pull. Lock the cup (ω=0) and the flower collapses to a plain ring at constant pull — the proof by touch.

self-test ✓ · closes ⟺ ω/Ω rational · petals = numerator · |a|∈[|RΩ²−ρ(Ω+ω)²|, RΩ²+ρ(Ω+ω)²] · locked cup PROVABLY a plain ring → 🏴‍☠️
The Swing-Ship
pump your own length, climb without a push · the Mathieu tongue at ωₘ=2ω₀

A swing climbs not because anything pushes it — but because the rider crouches (lengthens) at the bottom and stands (shortens) at the top, pumping the pendulum's length at twice its natural beat. That 2:1 timing lands in the principal Mathieu instability tongue and the arc grows on its own — parametric resonance. Time it right and you reach the loop; pump once per swing, same effort, and it refuses to grow. A phase coach lights the crouch/stand rhythm; the swing itself is the readout.

self-test ✓ · ω₀=√(g/L) exact · det(M)=1 exact · pump@2ω₀ → |λ|>1 (ln-amp linear, residual<1e-9) · pump@1ω₀ same ε PROVABLY bounded |λ|=1
