Face the decisive harvest: you're handed an open chain with another still waiting. Take the box and lose; hand it back and win.

Kin to The Matchbox That Learns and The Adversary — the same solved-games room, where here the verb is keep control. Carded, too, in The Numbers Room, among its solved games.

What's proven: the 3×3-dots board (4 boxes) is solved EXACTLY by retrograde minimax — every position's perfect-play margin is computed, not estimated. The green pill runs that solve live (it is the same code as the headless test). The long-chain rule is a published theorem (Berlekamp); here it is CHECKED against every solved sub-position we enumerate, and cross-checked by a second, independent oracle. A larger board (24+ edges) blows the search budget and carries no exactness claim — only the chain rule, checked where we can.
