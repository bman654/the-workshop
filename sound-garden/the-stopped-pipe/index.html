The Stopped Pipe

Blow a brass bore and it rings the full harmonic series, like the Plucked Reed. Now cap the far end. The reflection at the closed end flips sign — one sign flip on the same echo loop — and the pipe drops a clean octave while half its voices fall silent: the even partials wink out, leaving the hollow odd-only ladder (1, 3, 5, 7…) of a clarinet. The belly you watch doubles as the pitch you hear drops. (The ideal lossless cylinder — a pure straight bore, no flare, no end-correction; the evens land at the loop's noise floor, not a literal zero.)

Why capping one end drops an octave and kills the evens

A pipe is the Plucked Reed's echo loop: a burst of air seeds a delay line of length N = round(SR/f), and the loop reads it one lap back and feeds it forward through a tiny two-tap average. An open pipe reflects the wave back uninverted — the loop returns each lap with sign +1, repeats every N samples, and sounds the full n·f₁ series. Cap one end and the closed boundary inverts the reflection: the loop returns each lap with sign −1 (buf[i] = −1·g·(b·buf[i−N] + (1−b)·buf[i−N−1])). A signed loop only comes home after it flips twice — so the true period is 2N: the pitch drops a clean octave, and the antiperiodic boundary cancels every even partial, leaving the odd-only ladder.

The reveal: nothing was added or removed from the synthesis — just one reflection sign flipped. The belly you watch literally doubles (the page reads the loop over its true period, N open / 2N capped), and the even comb-pips wink out, as the octave falls in your ear. The honesty: this is the ideal lossless cylinder — a pure straight bore with no flare, no conical taper, and no end-correction. The cancelled evens land at ~5e−4 of the odds (tens of dB down), at the loop's noise floor, not a literal zero; a real clarinet's even partials are weak, not wholly absent.

self-test —

The self-test proves it EXACT: the open/capped fundamental ratio is 2.000000 at two degrees (only the FRACTIONAL period lands it); the capped pipe rings the ODD ladder while the evens sit at ~5e−4 of the odds; the OPEN pipe at the same length & blow re-derives the FULL series (so the cap removed the evens, not the synthesis); the capped loop is ANTIPERIODIC at lag N (acorr@N ≈ −1) while the open loop is periodic (≈ +1); and the recovered capped fundamental matches the closed-form c/4L within 10¢.
