The Comma

Stack twelve true perfect fifths and you do not land home — you overshoot the octave by a hair you can hear: the Pythagorean comma. Equal temperament's beautiful lie is to shave every fifth by the same tiny amount so the spiral snaps shut.

The wolf wedge — the gap made geometric

Each fifth climbs the clock by seven semitones, folding back into one octave. In justice the spiral creeps outward and the twelfth spoke lands short of a full turn — the filled gilt wedge is the comma's angle. Pull toward temper and the wedge shrinks to nothing: the spiral snaps onto a single clean ring — the 12-spoke circle of fifths, perfectly closed.

self-test —

The comma is exact: (3/2)¹² = 531441/4096, folded into one octave = 531441/524288 ≈ 1.013643 = 23.460 cents. Equal temperament narrows each fifth by exactly comma/12 = 1.955¢ — twelve of those close with zero residual. The self-test proves the gap is a property of the 3/2 ratio (the negative control: ET fifths close; just fifths do not), and that all pitch/ratio math is the single-sourced pitch-core.mjs byte-for-byte.
