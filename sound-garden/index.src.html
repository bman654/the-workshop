Four benches that take a sound apart

The garden mostly plays; these four reason. Each proves one law of how a vibration is built, bounded, or shared — the bass pipes of the rack, tuned an octave down. Sweep across them and you hear the wall run down to the low brass.

▶ strum The Butterfly the FFT laid on a wheel: any sound falls apart into its pure tones, one bin to a spoke. ▶ strum The Singing Glass sweep a pure tone past a wine glass and hear it wake only at its own note; resonance, made audible. ▶ strum The Sampling Theorem Nyquist's line: the exact rate below which a captured tone lies about its pitch. ▶ strum The Trading Bench two coupled pendulums pour one swing back and forth, beat for beat (kin of The Beating Bench).
