The Comb

Add a sound to a short-delayed copy of itself and the spectrum grows a comb of notches — the echo cancels itself wherever it lands a half-cycle out of phase. Drag the delay τ and the teeth slide: the first notch sits at 1/(2τ), the teeth spaced 1/τ apart. The comb you SEE is the comb you HEAR — one lever drives both, and as it sweeps you hear the jet-plane flange whoosh.

Why a comb appears (and where the teeth land)

Add a signal to a copy delayed by τ: y(t) = x(t) + g·x(t−τ). Its transfer is H(f) = 1 + g·e−j2πfτ, so the magnitude response is a cosine ripple, |H(f)|² = 1 + 2g·cos(2πfτ) + g². It peaks where the echo lands in phase (f = n/τ) and dips where it lands a half-cycle out of phase (cos = −1). The dips are the teeth.

The notch ladder: the dips sit at f = (n+½)/τ — the first notch at exactly 1/(2τ), the teeth spaced 1/τ apart, independent of g. The echo depth g sets only how DEEP they cut: the notch floor is |1−g|, a true zero at g=1 (total cancellation). The negative control: set τ=0 and the echo lands on top of the original — y = (1+g)·x, a flat gain, no teeth. The comb is the delay.

Two stated settings. The LIVE rig sweeps τ and g freely over pink noise or a tone. The headless heard-claim is made by verify.sh on the SAME delay-and-add at a FIXED probe (window.__renderComb: tones parked on the peaks n/τ and notches (n+½)/τ) so every checked tooth clears the Audio Lens's 60 Hz floor, 5 kHz ceiling and 3% dedup — the live UI's τ/g are NOT used by that render.

self-test —

The self-test proves it EXACT: the first notch is 1/(2τ) and the spacing 1/τ to the bit across a τ grid; the magnitude |H|² matches |1+g·e−j2πfτ|² by two independent computations; a rendered sum-of-tones probe is annihilated on the notches and doubled on the peaks; the depth floor is |1−g| (a true 0 at g=1) while the notch FREQUENCIES never move with g; and at τ=0 (or g=0) the spectrum is dead flat. The physics is single-sourced to core.mjs, byte-twinned into this page, with the Node twin core.test.mjs proving char-for-char parity.
