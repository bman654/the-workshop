The Vowel Throat

A vowel is not a note — it is a shape carved into a buzz. Hold a constant glottal hum, then drag one dot through vowel-space: two resonances slide along the harmonic comb and the same toneless buzz becomes “ah · ee · oo · oh”. Nothing is added — the throat CARVES the voice out of what was already there. This is the Overtone Rack's subtractive mirror: the Rack ADDS partials to build a tone; the Throat SUBTRACTS bands to uncover a voice.

Why it is a voice, not a note

The source is one glottal buzz — a sawtooth at f₀ ≈ 120 Hz, a FULL harmonic comb (every rung n at 1/n). The throat is two parallel bandpass resonances — the formants F1, F2. Their product carves the comb: rungs near a formant survive, the rest are attenuated. The surviving two clusters ARE the vowel — you can read them straight off the comb.

The mirror: the Overtone Rack ADDS partials to build a tone; here energy is only ever REMOVED. A vowel is what is LEFT. The honest test: a comb can only resolve to its nearest rung, so the two formant peaks recover the published F1/F2 to within ±one comb spacing (±f₀). The negative control: collapse both filters to one center and the two peaks merge — the two-formant classifier returns FALSE, and the vowel dissolves to a featureless hum. (Formant values: Peterson & Barney 1952, adult-male means.)

self-test —

The self-test proves it EXACT: two formants recover the published F1/F2 for /a/ and /i/ within ±one comb spacing; collapsing both resonances to one center MERGES the peaks and the two-formant classifier fails; the four vowels /a/ /i/ /u/ /e/ are four separated points in (F1,F2); and the throat only REMOVES energy — the source comb is FULL, the in-band envelope towers over the carved-out region. The physics is single-sourced to core.mjs, byte-twinned into this page, with the Node twin core.test.mjs proving char-for-char parity, a disjoint grid re-derivation of the bandpass peak, and that the laws are re-typed nowhere.
