Why a comb appears (and why the carrier nulls)

Wobble a carrier's phase with a modulator of depth β and the signal is y(t) = cos(2πfct + β·sin(2πfmt)). The Jacobi–Anger identity turns that one wiggling tone into an infinite sum of steady tones: y = Σn Jn(β)·cos(2π(fc+n·fm)t). So the spectrum is a comb: a rung at the carrier and a symmetric pair of sidebands at every fc ± n·fm, each |Jn(β)| tall. Energy is conserved — Σn Jn(β)² = 1 for every β.

The carrier null: the carrier's own height is J₀(β), which is 1 at β=0 and then oscillates, crossing zero at β = 2.404825557… (the first zero of J₀). At THAT depth the center rung vanishes — the loudest tone goes silent while the sidebands flood. The negative control: set β=0 and Jn(0) is 1 for the carrier and a true 0 for every sideband — one pure rung, an empty axis. The comb is the depth.

Two stated settings. The LIVE rig sweeps fc/fm/β freely. The headless heard-claim is made by verify.sh on the SAME FM chain at a FIXED lens band (window.__renderSidebands: fc=1200, fm=500) so every checked tooth clears the Audio Lens's 60 Hz floor, 5 kHz ceiling and 3% dedup — the live UI's fc/fm/β are NOT used by that render.

self-test —

The self-test proves it EXACT: each sideband amplitude is |Jn(β)| by two independent algorithms (downward recurrence vs power series) to <1e−9; energy is conserved Σn Jn² = 1 across a β-sweep; the carrier null is a real zero of J₀ (|J₀(2.4048…)| < 1e−9); the rendered FM comb reads |Jn(β)|/2 at every tooth; and at β=0 every sideband is a true 0. The physics is single-sourced to core.mjs, byte-twinned into this page, with the Node twin core.test.mjs proving char-for-char parity and cross-checking the first J₀ zero against the plate tool.
