Gate SFX Render Bench — dev harness, not forged, not linked from the gate

Call renderWav(soundKey, durSec) from the console (or via agent-browser eval). It loads audio-<soundKey>.js on demand, renders the builder through an OfflineAudioContext at 22050 Hz, encodes a 16-bit mono PCM WAV, drops the base64 into #wavout, and resolves the base64 string.
