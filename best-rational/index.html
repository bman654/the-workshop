how to steer it
The glowing jaw is the side the target lies on — press it (or the matching ← / → key) to take one mediant step and slam the window tighter. Press the wrong jaw and it just shakes. Each convergent you pass flashes gold and stamps into the ledger — it is the closest fraction to x of any denominator up to qₙ, checked live by brute force.
the punchlines
φ = [1;1,1,1,…] — every term is the smallest one can be, so its convergents (the Fibonacci ratios 1,2,3,5,8,13…) crawl in slowest. That makes φ the most irrational number — and the reason sunflower seeds sit at the golden angle: it's the angle hardest to approximate by a tidy fraction, so the seeds never line up into gaps.
