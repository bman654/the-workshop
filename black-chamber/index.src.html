A Black Chamber was a state's cipher-cracking office. This one is the workshop's analyst: give it ciphertext with no key, and it recovers the key and the message by classical cryptanalysis — frequency analysis, the index of coincidence, and a hill-climb on an English language model. It is the counterpart to the Volvelle and Scytale, which make the ciphers it breaks.

 

How it reads. The bars compare this text's letter frequencies against English. A flat profile and a low IoC betray a Vigenère; an English-shaped profile means a Caesar or substitution. Watch the plaintext below resolve from gibberish to English as the hill-climb finds the key.
