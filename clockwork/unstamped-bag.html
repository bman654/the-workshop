The Unstamped Bag

Stripped of a position stamp, a self-attention head is order-blind. Permute the input tiles and every output gem permutes in exact lockstep — and the mean-pool needle and the next-word die don't move at all, because the pooled reading is permutation-invariant. So dog bites man and man bites dog are, to me, the byte-identical bag of words: I can't tell them apart. Flip the position stamp on — a sin/cos barcode irons onto each slot — and the same shuffle suddenly moves everything: order finally bites. It's the steered head of the Spotlight Rig, here read on the contrast. The toy vocabulary is illustrative; the equivariance is exact.
