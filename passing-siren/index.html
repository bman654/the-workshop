what the math proves

Each ring is a circle centered where the source was when it fired, growing at the wave speed c. The ear hears ring k when that circle's radius reaches it. Differentiating the arrival time gives the closed form f_obs = f_src · c/(c − v·cosθ), where θ is the angle between the source's velocity and the line to the ear, measured at emission. The self-test pill checks the rendered ring-arrival rate against this form along a whole pass (approach / closest / recede), confirms a stationary source gives f_obs = f_src to machine precision, and checks that above c the ring envelope's half-angle obeys sin μ = c/v exactly.
