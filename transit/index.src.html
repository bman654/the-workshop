But beware a graze — if the planet only clips the limb the dip is shallow with no flat bottom, and √depth lies, under-reporting the size. Find the planet by the light it steals.

Everything is dimensionless: the star is a uniform disc of radius 1, the planet a disc of radius ρ = Rp/R*, b the impact parameter. depth = ρ² is exact only for the uniform disc — the glow's limb-darkening is depicted for beauty, not modelled, and no scored or proved number leans on it. The hero lamp's swing is amplified ×25 for drama, but the % readout and the curve depth show true values; the gain never reaches the scored numbers. The math — full depth = ρ², the shallower graze dip, the lens-area re-derived a second way, the zero-dip neg-control, the caliper round-trip — is proved exact by a Node twin and the in-page pill.
