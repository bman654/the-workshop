Lay two line-gratings over each other and a third pattern swims up — one that neither grid contains. It is the spatial beat of two combs: where the lines drift in and out of step, a slow bright-and-dark fringe appears. Drag to slide the top grating; turn the dial to rotate it. The fringe spacing obeys a closed law — and the bench FFTs the very brightness you see to prove it.
